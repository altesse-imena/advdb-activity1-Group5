2. Data dictionary

MEMBERSHIP_TYPE

Catalogue of membership plans offered by the gym (e.g. Basic, Standard, Premium).

AttributeKeyData typeDefinitionValidation rules
membership_type_idPKINTUnique identifier of a membership plan.Auto-increment; NOT NULL.
type_nameVARCHAR(30)Name of the plan.NOT NULL; UNIQUE.
monthly_feeDECIMAL(8,2)Current price charged per month for the plan.NOT NULL; ≥ 0.
duration_monthsSMALLINTMinimum contract length in months.NOT NULL; between 1 and 36.
access_levelVARCHAR(20)Level of facility access included.IN ('Off-Peak', 'Full', 'All-Access').
descriptionVARCHAR(255)Free-text summary of plan benefits.Optional.

MEMBER

A person registered with the gym who holds a membership plan.

AttributeKeyData typeDefinitionValidation rules
member_idPKINTUnique identifier of a member.Auto-increment; NOT NULL.
membership_type_idFKINTPlan currently held by the member.NOT NULL; references MEMBERSHIP_TYPE.
first_nameVARCHAR(50)Member's given name.NOT NULL; letters, spaces, hyphens only.
last_nameVARCHAR(50)Member's family name.NOT NULL.
emailUQVARCHAR(100)Contact e-mail address; also used for login.NOT NULL; UNIQUE; must match name@domain format.
phoneVARCHAR(20)Contact phone number.Digits, spaces, '+' and '-' only.
date_of_birthDATEMember's date of birth.NOT NULL; member must be ≥ 16 years old at join_date.
ageDerivedINTMember's current age in years.Not stored; computed from date_of_birth and today's date.
genderCHAR(1)Self-reported gender.Optional; IN ('F', 'M', 'X').
street_addressVARCHAR(100)Street and house number.Optional.
cityVARCHAR(50)City of residence.Optional.
postal_codeVARCHAR(10)Postal / ZIP code.Optional; alphanumeric.
join_dateDATEDate the member first registered.NOT NULL; DEFAULT current date; not in the future.
statusVARCHAR(10)Current membership state.NOT NULL; IN ('Active', 'Frozen', 'Expired', 'Cancelled').

PAYMENT_METHOD

Lookup list of accepted ways to pay.

AttributeKeyData typeDefinitionValidation rules
payment_method_idPKINTUnique identifier of a payment method.Auto-increment; NOT NULL.
method_nameUQVARCHAR(30)Name of the method (Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Mobile Money).NOT NULL; UNIQUE.
is_activeBOOLEANWhether the method is currently accepted.NOT NULL; DEFAULT TRUE.

PAYMENT

A single payment made by a member for a membership period.

AttributeKeyData typeDefinitionValidation rules
payment_idPKINTUnique identifier of a payment.Auto-increment; NOT NULL.
member_idFKINTMember who made the payment.NOT NULL; references MEMBER.
payment_method_idFKINTHow the payment was made.NOT NULL; references PAYMENT_METHOD.
processed_byFKINTReceptionist who recorded the payment.NOT NULL; references RECEPTIONIST(staff_id).
amountDECIMAL(8,2)Amount actually paid (historical value, kept even if the plan fee changes later).NOT NULL; > 0.
payment_dateDATETIMEDate and time the payment was received.NOT NULL; DEFAULT current timestamp.
period_startDATEFirst day covered by the payment.NOT NULL.
period_endDATELast day covered by the payment.NOT NULL; period_end ≥ period_start.
transaction_refUQVARCHAR(40)Receipt or bank/card transaction reference.UNIQUE when present; required for non-cash methods.
payment_statusVARCHAR(10)Outcome of the payment.NOT NULL; IN ('Pending', 'Completed', 'Failed', 'Refunded').

STAFF (supertype)

Any employee of the gym. Holds attributes shared by all staff; specialised into TRAINER or RECEPTIONIST.

AttributeKeyData typeDefinitionValidation rules
staff_idPKINTUnique identifier of an employee.Auto-increment; NOT NULL.
first_nameVARCHAR(50)Employee's given name.NOT NULL.
last_nameVARCHAR(50)Employee's family name.NOT NULL.
emailUQVARCHAR(100)Work e-mail address.NOT NULL; UNIQUE; valid e-mail format.
phoneVARCHAR(20)Work phone number.Digits, spaces, '+' and '-' only.
hire_dateDATEDate the employee started.NOT NULL; not in the future.
staff_typeCHAR(1)Subtype discriminator.NOT NULL; IN ('T', 'R'). Disjoint + total: every staff row has exactly one matching subtype row.

TRAINER (subtype of STAFF)

A staff member who leads fitness class sessions.

AttributeKeyData typeDefinitionValidation rules
staff_idPK, FKINTIdentifier shared with the STAFF supertype.NOT NULL; references STAFF; parent staff_type = 'T'.
specialtyVARCHAR(50)Main area of expertise (e.g. Yoga, HIIT, Strength).NOT NULL.
certificationVARCHAR(60)Highest fitness qualification held.NOT NULL.
hourly_rateDECIMAL(6,2)Pay rate per hour taught.NOT NULL; > 0.

RECEPTIONIST (subtype of STAFF)

A front-desk staff member who registers members and processes payments.

AttributeKeyData typeDefinitionValidation rules
staff_idPK, FKINTIdentifier shared with the STAFF supertype.NOT NULL; references STAFF; parent staff_type = 'R'.
shift_patternVARCHAR(20)Usual working shift.NOT NULL; IN ('Morning', 'Afternoon', 'Evening', 'Rotating').
desk_extensionVARCHAR(6)Internal phone extension at the front desk.Optional; digits only.

FITNESS_CLASS

A type of group class offered by the gym (the template for scheduled sessions).

AttributeKeyData typeDefinitionValidation rules
class_idPKINTUnique identifier of a class type.Auto-increment; NOT NULL.
class_nameVARCHAR(50)Name of the class (e.g. Spin 45, Morning Yoga).NOT NULL; UNIQUE.
descriptionVARCHAR(255)What the class involves.Optional.
categoryVARCHAR(30)Class family.NOT NULL; IN ('Cardio', 'Strength', 'Mind-Body', 'Dance', 'Aquatic').
difficulty_levelVARCHAR(12)Intended fitness level.NOT NULL; IN ('Beginner', 'Intermediate', 'Advanced').
duration_minutesSMALLINTLength of every session of this class.NOT NULL; between 15 and 180.
max_capacitySMALLINTMaximum participants per session.NOT NULL; > 0; must not exceed the capacity of the facility used.

FACILITY

A room or area of the gym where class sessions take place.

AttributeKeyData typeDefinitionValidation rules
facility_idPKINTUnique identifier of a facility.Auto-increment; NOT NULL.
facility_nameUQVARCHAR(50)Name of the room/area (e.g. Studio A, Pool).NOT NULL; UNIQUE.
room_typeVARCHAR(30)Kind of space.NOT NULL; IN ('Studio', 'Pool', 'Gym Floor', 'Outdoor').
capacitySMALLINTSafe maximum number of people.NOT NULL; > 0.
floor_levelSMALLINTBuilding floor where the facility is located.Optional; between -2 and 10.

CLASS_SESSION (Schedule)

One scheduled occurrence of a fitness class, at a date/time, in a facility, led by a trainer.

AttributeKeyData typeDefinitionValidation rules
session_idPKINTUnique identifier of a scheduled session.Auto-increment; NOT NULL.
class_idFKINTClass being run.NOT NULL; references FITNESS_CLASS.
trainer_idFKINTTrainer leading the session.NOT NULL; references TRAINER(staff_id); a trainer cannot lead two overlapping sessions.
facility_idFKINTRoom where the session is held.NOT NULL; references FACILITY; no two overlapping sessions in the same facility.
session_dateDATEDate of the session.NOT NULL.
start_timeTIMEStart time of the session.NOT NULL; within opening hours (05:00–22:00).
end_timeDerivedTIMEEnd time of the session.Not stored; start_time + FITNESS_CLASS.duration_minutes.
available_spotsDerivedINTPlaces still free in the session.Not stored; max_capacity − COUNT(ENROLLMENT rows for the session).

ENROLLMENT (weak / associative)

Resolves the M:N relationship between MEMBER and CLASS_SESSION; one row per member booked on a session.

AttributeKeyData typeDefinitionValidation rules
member_idPK, FKINTMember who booked the session.NOT NULL; references MEMBER; member status must be 'Active'.
session_idPK, FKINTSession that was booked.NOT NULL; references CLASS_SESSION; bookings cannot exceed max_capacity.
enrollment_dateDATETIMEWhen the booking was made.NOT NULL; DEFAULT current timestamp; must be before the session starts.
attendance_statusVARCHAR(10)Whether the member attended.NOT NULL; DEFAULT 'Booked'; IN ('Booked', 'Attended', 'No-Show', 'Cancelled').
